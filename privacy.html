Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 31, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when requesting a business consultation or filling out a form. This information includes:

  • Full Name: To address you correctly during consultation.
  • Company / Business Name: To conduct strategic research before our conversation.
  • Contact Details: Email address and Phone / WhatsApp number to reach you.
  • Business Context: Description of challenges, goals, and answers to forms.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected details to prepare, schedule, and execute your free business consultation. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Review and analyze your online brand presence.
  • Deliver custom diagnostics and growth recommendations.
  • Communicate scheduling updates via WhatsApp, Email, or phone call.
  • Improve the customer experience and accuracy of our growth solutions.

3. Data Protection and Security

We implement appropriate physical, technical, and administrative security protocols to safeguard your business details from unauthorized access, modification, or exposure. Your strategic operational information is kept confidential and only reviewed by the immediate Edition Finder consulting team.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute your business details, contact information, or strategic audit data to third-party advertisers, vendors, or marketing platforms. Your information is only shared with partners when explicitly authorized by you to coordinate capability execution.

5. Cookies and Tracking

Our website utilizes standard session-based cookies and analytics tracking solutions to measure site traffic patterns and responsiveness. You can adjust your browser parameters to disable cookies, although some layout features may degrade as a result.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us directly through the Contact Page.
